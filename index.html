ABOUT

Learning by doing.

I’m interested in cybersecurity because the strongest learning happens when theory meets real security telemetry, systems and investigation.

My background is in Computer Science with a specialization in Artificial Intelligence, and I’m currently pursuing an MSc in Cyber Security and Emerging Threats at Middlesex University Dubai.

Outside the classroom, I use labs and security projects to understand how attacks appear in systems and networks — and how defenders can detect, investigate and respond to them.

My current interests include SOC operations, SIEM and log analysis, threat detection, network traffic investigation, vulnerability assessment and penetration testing.
